Biography of Tzeke000

Introduction

Ezekiel, known professionally as Tzeke000, is a dynamic and innovative music artist who has captivated audiences with his unique blend of genres and powerful storytelling. Raised in the Charlotte, North Carolina area, Tzeke000's musical journey began at a young age, inspired by a diverse array of influences ranging from classic rock and contemporary pop to classical music.

Early Life

Tzeke000 was born and raised in the Charlotte area. From a young age, he was influenced by classical music and instruments like the piano and tuba.

Musical Journey

Tzeke000 began his musical career in 2016 and released his first song, “Çrønexa,” on SoundCloud in 2019. His first album, “Dub Brain,” arrived on all major streaming services in 2020.

Influences

Tzeke000 draws inspiration from artists such as Marshmello, Flume, San Holo, Au5 and deadmau5, and from genres including Future Bass, EDM and pop.

Personal Touch

Outside of music, Tzeke000 enjoys gaming, airsoft and working on his car. He believes that when the game is over, the king and the pawn go back into the same box.
