Bio

Tzeke000 (Ezekiel) is a Future Bass, EDM and pop artist from the Charlotte, North Carolina area. Classically trained on piano and tuba, he began releasing music in 2019 and dropped his debut album Dub Brain across all major platforms in 2020. His sound blends melodic, emotive drops with pop sensibility — drawing on influences like Marshmello, Flume, San Holo, Au5 and deadmau5.
